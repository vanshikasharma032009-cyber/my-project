Our Journey

Established in 1999, Bal Vikas Global School has been a cornerstone of quality education in Kaithal, Haryana for over two decades. What began as a modest initiative to provide accessible education has blossomed into a premier CBSE-affiliated institution serving 1500+ students from Nursery to Class 12.

Our founders envisioned a school that would combine academic rigor with character development, creating not just successful students but responsible citizens. Today, that vision continues to guide every decision we make.

Over the years, we have consistently achieved outstanding board results, with our students securing admissions in prestigious universities across India and abroad. Our alumni network includes engineers, doctors, entrepreneurs, and leaders in various fields.

Mission & Vision

The guiding principles that shape our educational philosophy.

Our Mission

To provide holistic education that nurtures intellectual curiosity, moral integrity, and social responsibility, empowering every student to achieve their full potential and contribute meaningfully to society.

Our Vision

To be a leading educational institution recognized globally for academic excellence, innovation in teaching, and the holistic development of future leaders who embody the values of compassion, courage, and creativity.

Core Values

  • Excellence - Striving for the highest standards
  • Integrity - Honesty in thought and action
  • Respect - Valuing diversity and dignity
  • Responsibility - Accountability to self and society
  • Innovation - Embracing change and creativity
