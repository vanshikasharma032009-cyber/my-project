Our Journey

Bal Vikas Global School is established and managed by Hari Om Education & Social Welfare Society. It is a co-educational, English-medium Senior Secondary school affiliated with CBSE, New Delhi (Affiliation No. 531726), offering education through Class 12 (10+2).

Since 2017, the school has expanded its facilities to support learning and student development. These include a Senior Division NCC wing, IT labs for coding and AI, and specialized coaching for NEET, JEE, and NDA.

At Senior Secondary level, students can pursue Science, Commerce, and Humanities streams.

Mission & Vision

The guiding principles that shape our educational philosophy.

Our Mission

To provide holistic education that nurtures intellectual curiosity, moral integrity, and social responsibility, empowering every student to achieve their full potential and contribute meaningfully to society.

Our Vision
