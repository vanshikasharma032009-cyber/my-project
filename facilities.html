Our Facilities

Home / Facilities

World-Class Infrastructure

A co-educational, English-medium CBSE Senior Secondary school (Affiliation No. 531726), with facility expansion since 2017.

Smart Classrooms

60+ digitally equipped classrooms with interactive smart boards, projectors, and high-speed internet for blended learning.

Science Laboratories

Fully equipped Physics, Chemistry, and Biology labs with modern apparatus, safety equipment, and separate prep rooms.

Computer & IT Labs

IT labs support coding and AI learning as part of the school's expanded facilities.

Digital Library

15,000+ books, e-books, journals, and digital resources. Quiet reading zones, research corners, and online database access.

Sports Complex

Indoor: Badminton, Table Tennis, Chess, Yoga. Outdoor: Cricket pitch, Football field, Basketball court, Athletics track, Skating rink.

Auditorium & Amphitheater

500-seat air-conditioned auditorium with professional sound/lighting. Open-air amphitheater for assemblies and performances.

Art, Music & Dance Studios

Dedicated rooms for visual arts, instrumental music, vocal training, classical & contemporary dance with mirrors and sound systems.

Safe Transport

GPS-enabled bus fleet with CCTV, female attendants, speed governors, and real-time parent tracking app covering 30+ routes.

Medical & Counseling

Full-time nurse, equipped infirmary, annual health checkups. Student counselor for emotional wellbeing and career guidance.

Senior Division NCC Wing

The school's Senior Division NCC wing is part of its facility expansion since 2017.

Cafeteria & Dining

Hygienic vegetarian kitchen serving nutritious meals. RO water plants on every floor. Separate dining for primary & senior students.

Security & Safety

24/7 CCTV surveillance, biometric entry, fire safety systems, earthquake-resistant structure, and regular safety drills.
