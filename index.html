Bal Vikas Global School is a co-educational, English-medium Senior Secondary school in Kaithal, Haryana. The school is affiliated with CBSE, New Delhi (Affiliation No. 531726), and offers education through Class 12 (10+2).

Since 2017, the school has expanded its facilities, including a Senior Division NCC wing, IT labs for coding and AI, and specialized coaching for NEET, JEE, and NDA.
