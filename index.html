Empowering Young Minds Since 1999

Bal Vikas Global School, established in 1999, has been a beacon of quality education in Kaithal, Haryana. Affiliated with CBSE, we offer a comprehensive curriculum from Nursery to Class 12, focusing on academic excellence, character building, and holistic development.

Our state-of-the-art campus spans across 5 acres, featuring modern classrooms, well-equipped laboratories, a digital library, sports facilities, and safe transportation. We believe in nurturing each child's unique potential through personalized attention and innovative teaching methodologies.

Our Academic Programs

Comprehensive curriculum designed for every stage of your child's educational journey.

Activity-based curriculum Phonics & early literacy Creative arts & music Outdoor play area

Primary (Class 1-5)

Strong foundation in core subjects with emphasis on conceptual understanding, critical thinking, and character development.
