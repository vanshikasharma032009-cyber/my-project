Admissions Open 2026-27

Home / Admissions

Join Our Family

We welcome applications for the academic session 2026-27 from Nursery to Class 12. Our admission process is designed to be transparent, fair, and child-friendly. We look for students who are curious, eager to learn, and will thrive in our vibrant community.

Admissions are granted based on age eligibility, availability of seats, and an interaction/assessment appropriate to the grade level. We encourage parents to visit our campus, meet our faculty, and experience our learning environment before applying.

1

Online Registration

Fill the enquiry form below or visit campus to collect the registration form. Submit with required documents.

2

Interaction / Assessment

Age-appropriate interaction with the child and parents. For Class 6+, a written assessment in English & Math.

3

Result & Admission Offer

Results communicated within 3-5 working days. Selected candidates receive admission offer letter.

4

Fee Payment & Enrollment

Complete fee payment within 7 days to confirm admission. Attend orientation program before session starts.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the admission timeline?

Admissions for 2026-27 open from October 2024. We recommend applying early as seats are limited. The process continues until seats are filled.

Is there an entrance test?

For Nursery to Class 5: Informal interaction only. For Class 6-10: Written assessment in English & Mathematics. For Class 11: Based on Class 10 board marks and stream-specific test if needed.

What about fee structure?

Fee structure varies by grade. Please contact our admissions office or visit the campus for detailed fee information. We offer sibling discounts and merit scholarships.

Is transport available?

Yes, we provide safe GPS-enabled bus transport covering Kaithal city and surrounding areas. Routes and charges are available at the school office.
